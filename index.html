About Project

You woke up on an unfamiliar ceiling, the bed is not the same. You looked around and had found yourself on an unfamiliar room. Why are you here? This is not your house. You have to get out.

You have 5 days to escape. Find items, solve puzzles, and find your way out of here. You must stay quiet and avoid making any noise. Granny hears everything. You must escape before its too late.

About Her

Granny

Granny is the main antagonist. She is crazy and keeps you trapped inside her house. She can hear everything and will follow the source of any noise.

You cannot tame her nor kill her. You must avoid her at all costs. If she catches you, you will be knocked out. However, in the fifth day... Let's just say she has already prepared something for you. You must escape before it's too late!

Gameplay

Explore the dark, creaky house. Search every drawer, cupboard, and hidden room for useful items. Solve puzzles, unlock doors, and carefully plan your escape route.

  • Search every drawer, cupboard, and hidden room for items
  • Solve puzzles and unlock doors
  • Stay silent — every dropped object can alert Granny
  • Escape within 5 days

Stay silent. Every footstep, every dropped object can alert Granny. She hears everything and will hunt you down the moment she detects a sound.

You only have 5 days. Use them wisely.
